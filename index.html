Property Details

A premium Boutique Homes build designed for growing families.

Set on a generous 571m² block in a convenient Manor Lakes location, this premium Boutique Homes build offers space, comfort and easy low-maintenance living.

Designed with growing families in mind, the home features four fitted bedrooms, including a master bedroom with private ensuite, plus a separate study/home office ideal for working from home, study or a quiet children’s homework area. Two separate living zones provide excellent flexibility for families, giving everyone space to relax, entertain or unwind.

At the heart of the home is a modern kitchen complete with stone benchtops, dishwasher and ample storage, connecting easily with the main living and dining area. The kitchen and living zone flow out towards the covered pergola, creating a practical indoor-outdoor entertaining space for family gatherings and weekend BBQs.

Outdoors, the property has been designed for easy living, with low-maintenance concrete areas, artificial grass, an automatic watering system and sensor lighting around the home.

Car accommodation is another major advantage, with a secure double garage plus side driveway/carport access with door and roller door, providing excellent flexibility for additional vehicles, trailer storage or work vehicles.

Additional features include ducted heating, evaporative cooling, downlights throughout, blinds throughout, alarm system, CCTV/security cameras, security sensors and a central family bathroom.

Located close to Manor Lakes schools, kindergarten, shops, library, train station, parks and private school bus pick-up/drop-off points, this is a move-in-ready opportunity for families, first home buyers, upsizers and investors seeking a spacious home in a well-connected location.
